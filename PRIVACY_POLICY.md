# Privacy Policy for Vatra Smoker Pro

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

Vatra Smoker Pro ("the App") is a free, open-source companion app for Bluetooth pellet grills.

## What the App collects
**Nothing.** The App does not collect, transmit, store or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission.

## Permissions and why
- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to find and talk to your grill. On Android 11 and older, Android ties Bluetooth scanning to the location permission; the App does not read or use your location.
- **Notifications:** to show live grill status and probe alarms.
- **Foreground service:** to keep monitoring the cook while the screen is off.
- **Wi-Fi permissions:** to let you send your home Wi-Fi name and password to the grill. They go straight from your phone to the grill over Bluetooth and are not saved or sent anywhere else.

## Data on your device
Temperatures, cook history and settings stay in the App's memory on your phone and are cleared when you close the App or clear its data in Android Settings.

## Children
The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
